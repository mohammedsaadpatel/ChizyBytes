Act as a world-class frontend developer, creative director, and award-winning web designer specializing in immersive, scroll-driven websites.

## MY TASK
Build a visually stunning, fully functional frontend website about:

**TOPIC: [INSERT THE GIVEN TOPIC HERE]**

The website should look like a premium Awwwards-winning interactive experience, with cinematic scroll animations, modern typography, beautiful compositions, and a strong visual identity that perfectly matches the topic.

### 1. CREATIVE DIRECTION
First, intelligently interpret the topic and create a unique visual concept around it. Do not use a generic template for every topic.

Choose appropriate:
- Color palette and visual atmosphere.
- Typography and font hierarchy.
- Backgrounds, imagery, illustrations, and visual elements.
- Animation style and transitions.
- Section layouts and storytelling approach.

The result must feel professionally art-directed, modern, immersive, and memorable.

### 2. HERO SECTION
Create a powerful, full-screen opening section featuring:
- A bold, oversized headline related to the topic.
- A short, compelling subtitle.
- A prominent call-to-action button.
- Beautiful background imagery, gradients, layered elements, or abstract visuals.
- Smooth entrance animations.
- Subtle parallax movement or animated background elements.
- A scroll indicator that encourages users to explore.

The first screen must immediately grab attention.

### 3. CINEMATIC SCROLL ANIMATIONS
Make scrolling the main attraction of the website.

Implement a variety of meaningful animations:
- Text reveals as sections enter the viewport.
- Images that slide, scale, or fade into view.
- Parallax backgrounds moving at different speeds.
- Sticky storytelling sections with changing text and visuals.
- Scroll-triggered transitions between sections.
- Animated counters or statistics where relevant.
- Horizontal scrolling galleries or timelines where appropriate.
- Smooth transitions between different visual scenes.
- Subtle hover interactions on buttons, cards, and navigation elements.

Animations must feel smooth, intentional, and connected to the content. Avoid excessive effects that make the site confusing or slow.

Use GSAP with ScrollTrigger if the environment supports installing dependencies. Otherwise, use CSS animations and the Intersection Observer API. Never sacrifice functionality just to include a library.

### 4. WEBSITE STRUCTURE
Design a complete experience with:
1. A minimal, stylish navigation bar.
2. An immersive hero section.
3. An introduction that explains the topic.
4. A visually engaging section highlighting key features, ideas, or benefits.
5. A creative scroll-driven storytelling section.
6. An interactive showcase, gallery, timeline, or comparison relevant to the topic.
7. A strong closing section with a call to action.
8. A polished footer.

Adapt the structure to the topic instead of forcing irrelevant sections into the design.

### 5. VISUAL QUALITY
Follow these design principles:
- Strong visual hierarchy and generous whitespace.
- Large, expressive typography.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.
- Carefully selected imagery.
- Layered compositions and depth.
- Sophisticated gradients, shadows, and borders when appropriate.
- Consistent corner radii and component styling.
- Beautiful loading and transition states where useful.
- No generic-looking cards everywhere.
- No excessive glassmorphism or unnecessary neon effects.
- No emoji used as a substitute for proper visuals.

Every section must look intentionally designed.

### 6. INTERACTIVITY
Implement working interactions:
- Responsive mobile navigation.
- Smooth scrolling to page sections.
- Functional buttons and calls to action.
- Interactive cards or tabs if relevant.
- Hover and focus states.
- Scroll progress indicator where appropriate.

Do not create fake buttons or nonfunctional controls.
