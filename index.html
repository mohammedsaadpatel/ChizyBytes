Chizy Bytes started in 2024 with a simple mission: serve fast food that doesn't compromise on taste or quality. Every burger is flame-grilled, every pizza is stone-baked, and every shawarma is hand-rolled with passion.

We source the freshest ingredients, use authentic spices, and make everything fresh to order. Whether you're a die-hard non-veg lover or a proud vegetarian — we've got your cravings covered!
